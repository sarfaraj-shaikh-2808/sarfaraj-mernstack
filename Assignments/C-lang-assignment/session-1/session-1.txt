

1. What is a Program?

  => A program is a set of instructions given to a computer to perform a specific task.
   Programs help us use different apps and websites. For example, in Instagram, a program
   allows us to upload photos, like posts, send messages, and watch reels.

3. Flowchart – Booking a Movie Ticket on BookMyShow

        START
          ↓
   Open BookMyShow App
          ↓
    Select Your City
          ↓
     Select a Movie
          ↓
    Select Date & Time
          ↓
   Select Cinema/Theatre
          ↓
     Select Seats
          ↓
     Confirm Seats
          ↓
    Make Payment
          ↓
     Payment Successful?
        ↙          ↘
      NO             YES
      ↓               ↓
 Try Payment Again   Booking Confirmed
      ↓               ↓
      └──────────→ Receive Confirmation
                       ↓
                      END



4. Algorithm – Ordering Food on Swiggy
=>
  1.Start.
  2.Open the Swiggy app.
  3.Enter or select your delivery location.
  4.Search for a restaurant or food item.
  5.Select a restaurant.
  6.Select the food items you want.
  7.Add the items to the cart.
  8.Check the cart and total amount.
  9.Select the delivery address.
  10.Choose a payment method.
  11.Make the payment.
  12.Check that the payment is successful.
  13.Receive the order confirmation.
  14.End.
