Question: Which development methodology would you recommend for this project, and why?
Identify two specific risks the team would face if they chose the wrong methodology for this
context.

Answer:

I recommend the Agile methodology for this project because the requirements change frequently and 
the client wants to review working features every two weeks. Agile allows the team to develop the 
project in small parts, take client feedback, and make changes quickly.

Two risks of choosing the wrong methodology:

1. Project delays: Using Waterfall may cause delays because changing requirements can require
   changes to completed stages.

2. Wasted time and money: The team may develop features that the client no longer needs, resulting
   in extra work and increased costs.
